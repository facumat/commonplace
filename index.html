Hola!

My name is Facu and this is my personal garden on the web where I document and house stuff about my work, art and interests. Hopefully, you find it enternaining or inspiring.

As a garden, this site is in constant flux. The colors on the leaves could change depending on the seasons or how much light they get, branches could twist one way or the other depending on my care, the whole thing might just wither away because I have come to neglect it.

I recently discarded my overtly trendy and functional webflow site for a handmade one due to ethical and conceptual reasons, you can learn more about the motives here.
