Organizing my resources

I was feeling like my design-related knowledge was disperse and unorganized: blogs I liked over there, my favorite podcasts on Spotify, the best inspo sites... in my memory? Stuff was scattered all over the digital and physical realm. The thing is, I am at ease when my knowledge is tidied up and secured somewhere I can go back to whenever the need arises. Hence the birth of Design Brain Food: a Notion space where I could save my design-related resources, organize it by media type, and categorize it in different sub-areas.

a bit of everything podcasts agencies courses influencers books inspo sites resources blogs tools

But why keep it to myself?

Realized that this hub of resources could be useful to many more people other than me: junior designers looking for places to start creating their own repertoire, enthusiasts that want to expand their vision, seniors looking for a model to store their resources. So, public it is then.

If you want your own, you can duplicate the space and create your personal DBF.

Pin it in your browser so you don't loose sight of it.

Set a goal to explore one new resource each day... or week if you're lazy.

Use it, share it, brake it, and if you have any feedback, send me an email!
